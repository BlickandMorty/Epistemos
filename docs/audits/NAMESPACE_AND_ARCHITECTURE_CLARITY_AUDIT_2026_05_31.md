## Build Contract

Epistemos has exactly two distributable builds:

1. MAS Build - App Store-safe public floor.
2. Pro Build - direct-distribution power build.

There is no separate Research build and no separate Vault build. Research,
Vault, Omega, heavy runtime, and future substrate work are Pro status bands:
Pro Live, Pro Gated, Pro Research, Pro Vault-Preserved, and Pro Omega.
