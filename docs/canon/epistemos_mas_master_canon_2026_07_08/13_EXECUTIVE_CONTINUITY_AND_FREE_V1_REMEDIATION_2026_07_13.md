## Governing decisions

1. **Free V1 is deterministic.** June, chat, general models, agents,
   generative actions, Browser, and ResearchHub are preserved paid source/data
   but completely hidden and inert in Free V1. Kokoro local read-aloud is the
   sole bundled/app-owned model exception. Owner-initiated macOS system
   dictation or text input may be admitted only as an explicit OS service; it
   may not add an app-bundled speech model, automatic transcription, provider,
   or background inference. Stored paid records may be parsed but must never
   create a Free V1 route, graph node, notebook tab, summary, setting, shortcut,
   deep link, restored state, provider startup, or background job.
2. **RECKONER uses real sources, not a substitute grid.** IronCalc is the
   intended visible Free V1 spreadsheet front end and sole formula/calc
   authority. Univer is a required bounded supporting source; it cannot
   replace IronCalc, compute formulas, or introduce a second active engine.
3. **MarkEdit is already present.** `LocalPackages/MarkEdit` is the vendored
   MIT source at `7d56e2e64322e983c43aa789bc08e238860f0069`. Do not re-clone
   its product shell. Improve Epistemos through its existing bridge while
   retaining the Epistemos palette and visual identity.
4. **No fake feature presentation.** Chat and unfinished Sheet/Datasheet
   controls disappear from Free V1 until their real deterministic feature
   slices exist. Preserve manifest/data compatibility; do not delete paid or
   future source merely to hide it.
5. **One executive edit owner.** Read-only audits may be delegated. The owner
   of an edit batch re-reads the cited source, owns tests/builds, records
   verification debt, and consolidates the next action. This document does not
   create a competing canonical execution key.
6. **Epdoc is the default rich-object lens.** Tasks/lists, calendar and reminder
   references, voice notes/audio, explicit dictation, drawings, images and
   attachments, meetings, PDFs, and datasets remain Markdown-plus-portable-
   artifact truth. Epdoc owns the full native visual/editable representation;
   Source and Prose share one typed native object inspector so unsupported or
   non-inline objects never disappear.
7. **PDF is the fifth canonical editor lens.** It is a full-size native PDFKit
   workspace in shared editor chrome, not a small preview. Epdoc-generated PDFs
   are derived renditions using the approved palette, registered fonts,
   hierarchy, images, and supported objects. PDFKit annotations/forms may be
   edited; structural body edits return to Epdoc/Source and regenerate. Imported
   PDF page content remains faithful and defaults to Save a Copy.
8. **Structure is dynamic, not a second note format.** Epdoc may expose document,
   outline/project, timeline/agenda/calendar, and object/attachment-sidebar
   views. Images and attachments are first-class portable referenced objects.
   Every view consumes the same derived manifest and preserves source reachability,
   dirty state, selection, scroll, undo, and KEELSTONE writes.
