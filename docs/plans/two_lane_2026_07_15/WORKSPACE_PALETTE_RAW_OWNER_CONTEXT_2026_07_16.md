## Reconciled design meaning — do not weaken these points

- **Palette.** “Sidebar” means a single, separate, compact app-level
  Workspace Palette window, not a sidebar copied into each workspace and not
  a full-window Home replacement. It has Now, Notes, Graph, and Settings. It
  is hide/show-able, remembers its state, is not always-on-top by default, and
  remains visually in the existing glass Notes Sidebar family.
- **Home.** The standalone Home stack is retired only after its useful
  greeting, commands, shortcuts, live workspace orientation, routing, note
  and graph capabilities are rehomed. There must be no hidden compatibility
  Home or dead stack afterwards.
- **Graph.** There is one durable Canonical Graph (one notes/entities/edges
  data authority) and many independent Graph View Sessions (camera, zoom,
  selection, filters, query, local layout/pins). A Palette graph and a
  multitask graph are two presentations of the same canonical data, not two
  graphs that couple one another’s local state and not duplicate databases.
  `Open in Multitask`, `Return/Move to Palette`, `Reveal Existing View`,
  `New Graph View`, and `Duplicate View` must say truthfully where a session
  currently lives.
- **Folder Graphs.** Folder context menu → Open Folder Graph makes a scoped
  view over stable folder identity and descendants, with Folder only versus
  Include connected context. It is temporary until explicitly saved and is
  honest if the source folder is renamed, moved, or deleted.
- **Graph responsiveness is a hard product requirement.** Creating a
  node/entity/relationship/tag/Epdoc child must be a minimal canonical diff
  propagated to relevant mounted sessions at a safe render boundary. It must
  appear naturally and immediately, preserve camera/selection/physics, and
  never ordinarily reload the entire graph, flash, freeze, clear an engine,
  recreate a renderer, or require closing/reopening a graph. The earlier
  observed reload/freeze/reopen failure is unacceptable, not a cosmetic issue.
- **Linking.** Retain Shadow IR/contextual-shadow intelligence and add direct
  shared Link/Relationship Composer controls. Calm Quick Link Suggestions are
  grounded in real candidates, explicit-accept only, cancellable/deduplicated
  while typing, consistently available in audited editors, and use the same
  canonical incremental mutation path as manual links. They may never silently
  mutate a note, tag, node, edge, or graph view.
- **Custom Graphs.** The Palette's canonical All Graph needs an explicit view
  switcher and durable Saved Graph Definitions: custom named workspaces over
  the real graph using scope, note-tag filters, keywords/query rules, pins,
  connection/boundary rules, graph-view labels, and optional layout. Users may
  create real tags/relationships through a view; those edits are canonical.
  Filters, selection, camera, local layout, pins, and hide-from-view are local
  unless explicitly saved. This is a real feature set, not merely a filter
  dropdown, but it never duplicates notes/entities/relationships.
- **Epdoc.** Epdoc is a genuine parent-notebook workspace: permanent Main
  Document tab, durable direct child documents and child notebooks, safe
  acyclic ownership and reparenting, breadcrumbs, real save/reopen behavior,
  calm material bottom tabs, and live pinned child-document cards at the top.
  Pinning is presentation/order, not ownership. The root Epdoc is a durable,
  prominent gold/yellow/symbol-distinct Canonical Graph node; child hierarchy
  is explicit rather than automatic top-level graph clutter. Every Epdoc/link/
  tag/child mutation follows the same incremental no-freeze graph path.
- **Menu bar.** The app remains a normal Dock/window app but has a useful
  Epistemos E identity and template-safe menu-bar icon. Its menu reveals real
  Palette/workspace/graph/capture/settings actions with truthful dynamic state;
  it does not preserve obsolete normal-user Home navigation or become a
  menu-bar-only product.
- **Live multitask and aliases.** Palette Now truthfully lists real open
  windows/workspaces and their relevant context. Selecting a row or invoking a
  persistent in-app alias focuses/reveals that exact existing surface rather
  than creating a duplicate. Closed/missing targets, collisions, rename, and
  restore behavior remain honest and safe. No Accessibility permission or
  global system-wide hotkey manager is introduced.
- **Settings.** Global settings live in Palette Settings, not ordinary
  multitask document tabs. Contextual graph/note controls remain in their
  workspace or inspector.
