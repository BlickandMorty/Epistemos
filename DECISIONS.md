## D-RES2 — LiteParsePDFConversion single-resume latch is @unchecked Sendable (2026-07-04)
RES-2 bounds the synchronous, uncancellable liteparse `importToMarkdown` FFI with a wall-clock timeout that ABANDONS the stuck conversion. "Abandon the loser" requires unstructured concurrency: structured task groups and `task.value` await their children at scope exit, so they cannot bound an uncancellable sync call (they would still hang on a truly-stuck parse). The race drives a `CheckedContinuation` resumed exactly once from whichever of {conversion, timeout, cancellation} settles first. `CheckedContinuation` is not `Sendable` and cannot cross an actor/task boundary, so `LiteParsePDFConversion.Gate` is a `final class: @unchecked Sendable` whose entire mutable state (settled/continuation/delivered) is serialized by one `NSLock`; correctness holds whether `attach` or `finish` runs first, and double-resume is impossible. Alternative rejected: an actor holding the continuation still requires sending the non-Sendable continuation across the actor boundary. File: Epistemos/LiteParse/LiteParsePDFConversion.swift; test: EpistemosTests/LiteParsePDFConversionTests.swift.
